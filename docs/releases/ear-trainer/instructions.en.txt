1. No network setup is needed. After startup, tap Up or Down to choose a training category, press OK to enter, then select Start.
2. Listen, tap Up or Down to choose an answer, and press OK to submit. Select Replay to listen again. Review the feedback and continue; chord breakdown plays the individual notes followed by the complete chord.
3. For rhythm practice, listen to the demonstration and four count-in beats, then press OK eight times during the silent section. Tap Up to replay or Down to end.
4. Volume starts at 90. Hold Up to increase it or Down to decrease it; keep holding to adjust continuously, with 0 for mute. In other exercises, hold OK to go back or end the session. Choose another round on the results page. Settings and scores are saved locally.
