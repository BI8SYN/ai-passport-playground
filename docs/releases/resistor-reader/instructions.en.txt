1. After installation, power on and start immediately; no network setup is needed. The initial yellow–violet–red–gold resistor reads 4.7 kΩ, ±5%. Enter band colors manually; the app does not recognize physical resistors automatically.
2. The small teal mark below the resistor shows the selected band. Press Up or Down to change its color. The resistance and tolerance range update as you adjust it.
3. Tap OK to select the next band. Hold OK for about 0.4 seconds to select the previous one. Releasing after a hold does not move forward again; continue using Up and Down to edit.
4. Select the band-count option at the top and use Up or Down to switch between 4, 5 and 6 bands. Tap OK to return to the bands and keep experimenting. Restarting restores the initial state.
